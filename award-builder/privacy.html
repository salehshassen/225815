Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Applies when published

Google sign-in, account records, download IP addresses and your designs: what we process and how you can contact us.

Who operates this service

Jaiza Studios is operated by Saleh Hassen. For privacy questions or requests, email mousasaleh003@gmail.com. This policy covers the Jaiza Studios website, Google sign-in, account administration and file-generation services.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile-picture URL, where provided. Authentication is handled through Supabase. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or calendars.

Account and usage records

We store your account ID, email, profile information, registration and last-login times, account status, assigned plan, export count, last-export time, and the IP address and time of your most recent official download (see below). Export records include a reference ID, file type, timestamp, file size and technical hashes. Administrative records may include changes to plans or account status, the reason for a change, and the administrator involved. Service providers may process IP addresses, browser information and technical logs for delivery, security and troubleshooting.

IP addresses and download restrictions

When you request an official download (a drawing, 3D model, cutting file, artwork, design record or ZIP package), our backend reads the IP address of your connection as reported by our backend provider’s network (Supabase). We use it only to check whether downloads from that address have been blocked and to help administrators stop misuse of the download service. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not look up your location from it.

We keep three kinds of IP-address records. First, the IP address and time of your most recent successful official download, stored with your account record. It is replaced at your next download and deleted together with your account. Second, IP addresses that an administrator has blocked, with their status (blocked or restored), the optional reason, the dates, the administrator involved and a count of download attempts refused from that address. These records are not attached to your account, but an IP address can still relate to a person, household or organization. Third, administrative log entries for each block and restore, including the IP address and reason.

A block applies to an address, not to a person. Other people who share the same network, such as a workplace, school, café or mobile carrier, may also be unable to download until the address is restored. A block affects official downloads only: you can still view the website, sign in and use previews. If you think a block is a mistake, email mousasaleh003@gmail.com.

Blocked-address records and the related administrative log entries remain until an administrator removes them; there is currently no automatic deletion period. You can ask about, or request deletion of, records relating to you as described below; we may need to keep a block record where it is necessary to prevent continued misuse.

Designs, inscriptions and logos

Previews are generated in your browser. When you request an official export, your design parameters, inscription and any included logo are sent to our Supabase backend to generate the requested files. The current application database stores export metadata rather than a saved library of full designs or generated files. Processing infrastructure may retain operational logs. Do not submit confidential or sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for the award. You decide whether to share downloaded files with a manufacturer; we do not automatically send them to manufacturers.

How we use information

We use information to sign you in, maintain your account, generate files, measure exports, enforce plan limits, administer access, block downloads from specific IP addresses when needed, prevent abuse, provide support and investigate technical problems. Authorized administrators can view account and usage records and export a user list for administration. We do not sell personal information, use Google user data for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Who receives information

Google provides sign-in, Supabase provides authentication, database and backend processing, and GitHub Pages hosts the public website. They process information needed to provide their services under their own policies and our applicable service arrangements. Authorized administrators access information as needed to operate the service. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service. We do not publish your account details in the public website repository.

Storage locations and security

The configured Supabase database region is Seoul, South Korea. Google, GitHub and other service infrastructure may process information in other countries. We use HTTPS and access controls, including account and administrator permissions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your Google account secure and sign out on shared devices.

Browser storage

The application uses browser local storage for drafts, preferences, approval state and sign-in sessions. This data can remain on your device until cleared. Clearing browser storage may erase local drafts and sign you out. The current application does not include advertising trackers or a separate marketing analytics service. Hosting and authentication providers may use their own essential technologies.

Retention and deletion

Account and export records remain while needed to provide your account and operate the service. The current application does not automatically erase records after a fixed period. IP-address records are described in the section on IP addresses above. You may request deletion by emailing us from your registered email address. After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize information no longer needed, subject to legal obligations and necessary security or dispute records. Provider logs and backups may persist according to their retention cycles. Local browser copies and files you downloaded or shared are not removed by account deletion.

Your choices and requests

Email mousasaleh003@gmail.com to request access, correction, a copy of your information, account deletion, or to raise a privacy concern. We may ask for proportionate identity verification. You can revoke Jaiza Studios access in your Google Account third-party connections settings. Revoking Google access does not itself delete existing Jaiza Studios records. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights, including objection, restriction or a complaint to a competent authority. We will address requests as required by applicable law.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change. Material changes, such as adding payment processing or new uses of personal data, will be explained before they take effect where required. Contact: mousasaleh003@gmail.com.
