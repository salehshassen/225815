Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Applies when published

Google sign-in, account records and your designs: what we process and how you can contact us.

Who operates this service

Award Builder is operated by Saleh Hassen. For privacy questions or requests, email mousasaleh003@gmail.com. This policy covers the Award Builder website, Google sign-in, account administration and file-generation services.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile-picture URL, where provided. Authentication is handled through Supabase. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or calendars.

Account and usage records

We store your account ID, email, profile information, registration and last-login times, account status, assigned plan, export count and last-export time. Export records include a reference ID, file type, timestamp, file size and technical hashes. Administrative records may include changes to plans or account status, the reason for a change, and the administrator involved. Service providers may process IP addresses, browser information and technical logs for delivery, security and troubleshooting.

Designs, inscriptions and logos

Previews are generated in your browser. When you request an official export, your design parameters, inscription and any included logo are sent to our Supabase backend to generate the requested files. The current application database stores export metadata rather than a saved library of full designs or generated files. Processing infrastructure may retain operational logs. Do not submit confidential or sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for the award. You decide whether to share downloaded files with a manufacturer; we do not automatically send them to manufacturers.

How we use information

We use information to sign you in, maintain your account, generate files, measure exports, enforce plan limits, administer access, prevent abuse, provide support and investigate technical problems. Authorized administrators can view account and usage records and export a user list for administration. We do not sell personal information, use Google user data for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Who receives information

Google provides sign-in, Supabase provides authentication, database and backend processing, and GitHub Pages hosts the public website. They process information needed to provide their services under their own policies and our applicable service arrangements. Authorized administrators access information as needed to operate the service. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service. We do not publish your account details in the public website repository.

Storage locations and security

The configured Supabase database region is Seoul, South Korea. Google, GitHub and other service infrastructure may process information in other countries. We use HTTPS and access controls, including account and administrator permissions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your Google account secure and sign out on shared devices.

Browser storage

The application uses browser local storage for drafts, preferences, approval state and sign-in sessions. This data can remain on your device until cleared. Clearing browser storage may erase local drafts and sign you out. The current application does not include advertising trackers or a separate marketing analytics service. Hosting and authentication providers may use their own essential technologies.

Retention and deletion

Account and export records remain while needed to provide your account and operate the service. The current application does not automatically erase records after a fixed period. You may request deletion by emailing us from your registered email address. After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize information no longer needed, subject to legal obligations and necessary security or dispute records. Provider logs and backups may persist according to their retention cycles. Local browser copies and files you downloaded or shared are not removed by account deletion.

Your choices and requests

Email mousasaleh003@gmail.com to request access, correction, a copy of your information, account deletion, or to raise a privacy concern. We may ask for proportionate identity verification. You can revoke Award Builder access in your Google Account third-party connections settings. Revoking Google access does not itself delete existing Award Builder records. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights, including objection, restriction or a complaint to a competent authority. We will address requests as required by applicable law.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change. Material changes, such as adding payment processing or new uses of personal data, will be explained before they take effect where required. Contact: mousasaleh003@gmail.com.
